tokenizer, codec or third-party license. LICENSES.txt accompanies each exported

Changes made for irodoru distribution
-------------------------------------
The speech checkpoint and codec are converted to ONNX, split into inference
graphs, and rewritten to portable ONNX operators. Weight normalization is
removed for export. Tokenizer JSON and forced split rules are exported for the
Rust engine. Deterministic codec encoding/decoding and the upstream Irodori
watermark bypass are used. These converted files are modified derivatives,
not unmodified upstream checkpoints. Do not represent exported audio as being
protected by the original DACVAE or SilentCipher watermark.

The license review covers published grants and notices. It does not establish
ownership or clearance of every training recording or grant rights to imitate
a person's voice. Follow the upstream usage conditions and obtain necessary
permissions for reference recordings and other supplied content.
